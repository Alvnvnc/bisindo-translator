Jemari

From fingertip signs to spoken words.

A real-time BISINDO (Indonesian Sign Language) alphabet translator that runs entirely on your device — built as a communication bridge at public service counters: clinics, village offices, banks, schools.

📷

Camera → keypoints

MediaPipe Hand Landmarker extracts 21 hand keypoints per frame, in-browser (WASM/WebGL).

🧠

Self-trained classifier

A small MLP (366 KB) trained on real BISINDO data, executed fully client-side — not an API call.

🔊

Text & Indonesian speech

Recognized letters build a transcript that can be spoken aloud via the Web Speech API — in Bahasa Indonesia, because that's who's listening at the counter.

🔒

Privacy by design

Video never leaves the device. No server, no API keys, no tracking, no analytics.

📖

Open & reproducible

MIT code, licensed public dataset, landmark data and evaluation metrics in the repo. Automated data audits included.

🤝

Built to be trusted

Controlled splits and benchmarks, open reports, and honestly acknowledged limitations in the docs.

Why “Jemari”?

Jemari means “fingers” in Bahasa Indonesia — because that is where sign language lives. The app reads those moving fingers and gives them a voice, anywhere, without an intermediary.
